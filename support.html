Frequently asked questions

My child can't log in — how does that work?

Children don't have accounts or passwords. Instead, they appear as profiles on the family iPad. After you (the parent) set up your family on iPhone, the same family is visible on any iPad signed in with the same Apple ID.

On the iPad, your child simply taps their name to enter their personal star board — no login required.

I don't see my family on the iPad

Make sure both devices are signed in with the same Apple ID. Open OurFamilyHub on the iPad — if you see a sign-in screen, tap Sign in with Apple using the same Apple ID you used on iPhone.

If you use Family Sharing, the iPad must be in the same Family Sharing group and signed in with the owner's Apple ID.

A star disappeared / the task shows red

A red task means it was rejected — it won't count until it's done again and re-approved. The child can tap it again after completing the task, and you can re-approve it in the parent panel on iPhone.

Stars that are pending approval are also not counted yet. They show with a ⏳ indicator until you approve or reject them.

The trophy didn't appear even though we hit the goal

Make sure the app is open on the device — trophy celebrations trigger when the app receives the update in real time. Try closing and reopening the app if the trophy shelf doesn't update.

If the issue persists, email us at magbjurling@gmail.com and we'll look into it.

How do I change a child's goal or reward?

On iPhone, go to Admin → Stars → tap the child's card → tap Edit. You can change the star goal (number of stars needed before the reward triggers) and the reward text.

How do I approve or reject a task?

When a child taps a task on the iPad, it enters a pending state. On iPhone you'll see a badge on the Admin tab. Tap the notification to see all pending approvals, then approve or reject with one tap. Rejected tasks show in red on the iPad and can be re-attempted.

What is the Morning Cycle?

The Morning Cycle is a family-synced check-in ritual. It guides the whole family through a sequence of categories (e.g. morning routine → tasks). Each child checks in for the current step; once everyone has checked in, the family moves on to the next step and bonus stars are awarded automatically at the end.

You can configure or disable it in Admin → Settings → Morning Cycle.

How do I remove or change the admin PIN?

On iPhone, go to Admin → Settings → Admin PIN. You can change or remove the PIN here. Face ID / Touch ID is offered as a faster alternative.

If you've forgotten your PIN, the only recovery path today is to email us. A self-service PIN reset will be added in a future update.

The family calendar isn't showing events

OurFamilyHub reads calendars named "Family" or "Familie" directly from your device using iOS Calendar. Check that:

  • There is a calendar with one of those names in the iOS Calendar app on the iPad.
  • OurFamilyHub has permission to access calendars: Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → OurFamilyHub.
  • If you use Google Calendar, the Google account must be added to iOS Settings → Calendar → Accounts.
The weather widget isn't working

The weather widget uses Apple WeatherKit and requires a physical device — it does not work in the iOS Simulator. Make sure location services are enabled for OurFamilyHub: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → OurFamilyHub.

How do subscriptions and billing work?

OurFamilyHub offers a free tier (2 child profiles, morning routine category) and optional premium subscriptions with more profiles and categories. All purchases and billing are handled by Apple through your App Store account.

To manage, pause, or cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → [Your name] → Subscriptions → OurFamilyHub. If a feature you paid for is not unlocked, try Restore Purchases in the app's subscription screen — it may take a few minutes to sync after purchase.

How do I delete my account and family data?

You can delete individual child profiles from Admin at any time. To permanently delete your account and all associated family data, email magbjurling@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" — we will erase all data within 30 days.

Quick tips for getting started

Add children first. On iPhone, tap the + button in Admin to add your first child profile. Set a star goal (e.g. 10) and a reward they'll love.
Default routines are ready to go. Morning and evening routines come pre-filled with steps. Delete what doesn't fit your family and add your own.
Set an admin PIN. A 4-digit PIN (or Face ID) prevents children from accidentally opening admin mode from the iPad.
The iPad is always-on. Leave the iPad plugged in and the app open — it will always show your children's star boards, family calendar, and photos.
