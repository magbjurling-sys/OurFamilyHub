Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to expand it. Still stuck? Email us.

Children don't have accounts or passwords. They appear as profiles on the family iPad. After you set up your family on iPhone, the same family is visible on any iPad signed in with the same Apple ID. Your child simply taps their name — no login required.

Make sure both devices are signed in with the same Apple ID. Open OurFamilyHub on the iPad — if you see a sign-in screen, tap Sign in with Apple using the same Apple ID you used on iPhone. If you use Family Sharing, the iPad must be in the same group.

A red task was rejected — it won't count until re-done and re-approved. Stars that are pending approval also aren't counted yet (⏳ indicator). The child can tap the task again after completing it, and you can approve it from iPhone.

Trophies are awarded automatically the moment a child crosses their goal. If a trophy doesn't appear right away, close and reopen the app to load the latest count. Still missing? Email us and we'll look into it.

On iPhone: Admin → Stars → tap the child's card → tap Edit. You can change the star goal and the reward text at any time.

When a child taps a task on the iPad, it enters pending state. On iPhone, open Admin → Approvals (a badge shows the count). Approve or reject each one with a single tap, or use Approve all to clear the whole list at once. Rejected tasks show red on the iPad and can be re-attempted the same day.

The Morning Cycle is a family-synced check-in ritual. The kids check in as they wake up, then move through your chosen categories one at a time — with a cheering dance scene during "Get dressed", a rising sun, and a tap-to-open gift box at the end. When everyone finishes, the round ends with a soft reward you define (a hug, a small choice, a surprise) rather than automatic stars — bonus stars are optional and default to 0. Set the order and rewards in Admin → Settings → Morning Cycle.

Go to Admin → Settings → Admin PIN. You can change or remove the PIN here. Face ID / Touch ID is offered as a faster alternative. You'll need to be signed in as the parent, so keep your code somewhere safe.

OurFamilyHub reads calendars named "Family" or "Familie" from iOS Calendar. Check that:

  • A calendar with one of those names exists in the iOS Calendar app on the iPad.
  • OurFamilyHub has calendar permission: Settings → Privacy → Calendars → OurFamilyHub.
  • For Google Calendar: the account must be added in iOS Settings → Calendar → Accounts.

Weather is provided by Apple Weather and needs location access: Settings → Privacy → Location Services → OurFamilyHub. It updates on its own once granted.

All purchases are handled by Apple through your App Store account. To manage, pause or cancel: iPhone Settings → [Your name] → Subscriptions → OurFamilyHub. If a paid feature isn't unlocked, try Restore Purchases in the app — it may take a few minutes to sync.

You can do this yourself in the app: Admin → Delete account removes your entire account and all family data. You can also delete individual child profiles from Admin at any time. Prefer email? Write magbjurling@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and we'll erase everything within 30 days.

Getting started

Quick tips for new families

➕

Add children first

On iPhone, tap the + button in Admin to add your first child. Set a star goal (e.g. 10) and a reward they'll love.

📋

Default routines are ready to go

Morning and evening routines come pre-filled. Delete what doesn't fit and add your own.

🔒

Set an admin PIN

A 4-digit PIN (or Face ID) prevents children from accidentally opening admin mode from the iPad.

🔌

Leave the iPad plugged in

The iPad is always-on — leave it plugged in and the app open so kids always see their star boards.
