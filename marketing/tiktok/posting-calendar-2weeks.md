## The weekly rhythm

- **Every post:** upload → paste caption → **pin the comment** (app name + "link in bio").
- **48h after each post:** glance at *completion rate* + *profile clicks* in TikTok analytics. Those predict installs; raw views don't.
- **End of Week 1:** whichever short has the best completion rate → that's your winner. In Week 2, re-film/re-cut 1–2 variants of it (new hook, same video).
- **End of Week 2:** take the overall winner → boost as a Spark Ad (ask me for the campaign brief).

## A/B test — opener (Short #3, the most important post)

Run the same video twice, ~4 days apart, with two different first lines. Whichever gets higher completion is your template going forward.

**Variant A (pain-first):**
> The laminated fridge chart lasted us… 4 days 💀 this is the first thing that's actually stuck. What chore do you fight about most? 👇

**Variant B (curiosity-first):**
> Nobody tells you WHY chore charts fail — it's not your kids 👀 here's the fix that finally worked in our house. Tag a parent who needs this 👇
