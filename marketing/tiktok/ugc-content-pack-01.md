## 0. How to use this pack

1. Pick a video concept below (start with #1 and #2 — strongest install hooks).
2. Send the **Creator Brief** to your UGC creator (or film it yourself).
3. When the video is ready, copy the **Caption + Hashtags + Mentions** straight into TikTok.

> **Why UGC for installs:** native-looking parent videos beat polished ads on TikTok. The algorithm and the audience both reward "real mom/dad in their actual kitchen" over branded production. Every concept below is written to look like organic content, not an ad.

## 1. Voice & rules (read once)

- **Persona:** a real parent talking to other parents. Warm, a little tired, honest, funny. Never corporate.
- **First 1.5 seconds = everything.** The hook must land before anyone scrolls.
- **Show the pain before the product.** Relatability first, app second.
- **One idea per video.** Don't explain every feature — sell *one* outcome.
- **Always end on the same CTA** so it compounds: *"It's called OurFamilyHub — link's in my bio."*
- **Captions:** TikTok favors 1–2 punchy lines + a question to drive comments (comments = reach).
- **Sound:** use a trending audio when possible; for talking-head, original audio is fine.
- **No fake claims, no fake reviews.** Honesty converts better and keeps the account safe.

---

## 2. Hashtag strategy

Don't spam 30 tags. Use **4–6**: a mix of broad + niche + intent.

**Core set (rotate, pick 4–6 per post):**
```
#momsoftiktok #parentinghacks #gentleparenting #chorechart #kidschores
#momhack #toddlermom #boymom #familyroutine #screentime #allowance
#morningroutine #parentinghack #momlife #dadsoftiktok #positiveparenting
```

**Rules:**
- 1–2 BIG (#momsoftiktok, #parentinghacks) for reach.
- 2–3 NICHE intent (#chorechart, #allowance, #familyroutine) for qualified viewers who actually install.
- Skip #fyp / #foryou — they do nothing now and look amateur.

---

## 3. Mentions strategy ("as if a person")

- **Tag your own account** in caption when relevant: `made this with @ourfamilyhub`.
- **Tag the creator** (if UGC) so they reshare → free reach: `@creatorhandle`.
- **In comments, not caption:** answer "what app?" with the name + "link in bio". Tagging accounts you don't control in captions can look spammy — keep it natural.
- Do **not** mass-tag random big accounts; TikTok suppresses that.

## 7. Posting & testing plan (to actually get installs)

- **Cadence:** 3–5 posts/week for the first 3 weeks. Volume finds the winner.
- **Test by hook, not by feature.** Same app, different first 2 seconds. The hook is 80% of performance.
- **Read at 48h:** look at **watch-time / completion rate** and **profile clicks**, not just views. Profile clicks → bio → install is your funnel.
- **Double down:** any video over ~3–5k views with above-average completion → re-film 2–3 variants.
- **Bio = your store link.** Make sure the App Store link is in the TikTok bio; pin a "link in bio 👆" comment on every post.
- **Spark Ads next:** once an organic video wins, boost it as a Spark Ad to scale installs. (Ask me to build the campaign brief when you're ready.)
- **Measure:** your TikTok pixel already fires `ClickButton` on App Store clicks from the site. For in-app installs, add TikTok's App Store SKAdNetwork / events via TikTok Ads Manager when you start paid. (I can spec this.)
