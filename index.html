Rewards

Real rewards kids save toward

Give stars a real-world value: a ladder of rewards you decide on, and — if you like — real pocket money kids can cash in. Use whatever motivates your child.

🎁
Per child

Reward ladder

A ladder of named rewards that unlock based on how many stars a child has earned. Kids always see "Saving toward your next reward" with a progress bar — at their own pace.

  • 🍦Ice cream10 ⭐Unlocked
  • 🎮30 min screen time15 ⭐
  • 🍝Pick dinner25 ⭐
  • 🎬Cinema50 ⭐
  • 🎁Big surprise100 ⭐

Build it in seconds with suggestions, or start from a ready-made ladder.

💰
Per family · optional

Weekly allowance

Turn stars into a cash value. Each star is worth an amount you set, and the child builds a balance they can take out. Kids learn to save, you keep full oversight.

Cash out — from stars to real pocket money

Kids cash in saved stars for real money via Vipps, Venmo or PayPal. The child requests a cash-out on the board; you get a notification, open your own payment app, complete the payment and confirm.

Pay with your own app:
🔒

The app never moves money itself. It only opens your own payment app — you always complete the payment and confirm manually. Stars are deducted only when you confirm "sent". No card details, no payment license, full parental control.

New · Big-kid mode

Bigger kids? Their own board on their own phone.

Not everyone wants to share an iPad. A bigger child can use their own phone, linked to the family with a code from you — with their private board, and the option to suggest tasks. Still 100% parent-controlled.

📱

Their own phone, linked with a code

No Apple ID and no child account — just an anonymous device token you can unlink at any time.

🔒

A private, per-child board

The child sees their own stars, rewards and routines — plus weather, question of the day and the day plan. Never a sibling's data.

➕

Kids can suggest tasks

A suggestion from the child's phone comes to you for approval, then becomes a one-off task. You decide.

🔔

Their own push alerts

The child is notified when a reward or payout is approved — plus friendly reminders before tasks with a deadline.

Still no child account, still your control. You approve everything that matters, and unlink the device whenever you like.

New · Works with the screens you have

The family board — on any screen

Not everyone has a spare iPad. With widgets and StandBy, a phone becomes a little family board — and the Morning Round shows live while it's running.

🧩

Widgets

Child status (stars, streak, next reward), the day plan and pending approvals on the Home or Lock Screen. Pin to one child — a tap opens the right screen.

🌙

StandBy

Set the phone on its charger in landscape and it becomes a calm, ambient family board — no iPad required.

⏱️

Live Activity

The Morning Round's progress shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while it's running.
