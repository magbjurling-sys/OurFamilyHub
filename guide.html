OurFamilyHub rewards kids with stars for finishing routines and tasks — and lets you run the whole thing from your iPhone. This guide takes you from download to a working family in under 10 minutes.

The big idea

A star economy the whole family understands

Kids earn stars for things they do. Stars build toward a goal. Hit the goal and a trophy unlocks — plus a real-world reward you choose. Here's the loop.

1

Kid taps

On the iPad, your child taps a task they've finished in StarTap.

→
2

You approve

It lands in “Pending” on your iPhone. One tap to approve — or reject.

→
3

Star counts

Approved stars fill a board, grouped in fives, toward the goal.

→
4

Trophy unlocks

Reach the goal → a trophy is earned, the board resets, the cycle begins again.

💡

Why the approval step?

It keeps you in control and means kids can't “cheat” their way to stars. Rejected tasks show up red with a friendly “Try again” and can be redone the same day.

Three building blocks

Routines, Tasks & Cycles

They look similar, but each works differently. Knowing the difference makes setup obvious — and you'll only ever need a few of each.

📋

Routine

Parent-led · one kid

An ordered set of small steps that belong together — “Get dressed → Brush teeth → Eat breakfast.”

  • You lead the child through it, step by step
  • No waiting — you give the star on the spot
  • Lives in Admin → Routines
No approval needed
✅

Task

Kid-reported · independent

A single, standalone action — “Tidied my room.” The child decides when it's done.

  • Kid taps it in StarTap themselves
  • Needs your approval before it counts
  • Recurring (daily) or one-off
Approval required
🔄

Cycle

Family-synced · together

A shared check-in ritual across categories — like the “Morning Round” the whole family does in step.

  • Each kid checks in; progress is shared
  • Credited instantly, no approval
  • Bonus stars when everyone finishes
Instant check-in
🧭

Rule of thumb

A guided walk-through of several steps → Routine. Something the kid reports and you approve → Task. Something the whole family does together, in sync → Cycle.

Part 1 · On your iPhone

Parent setup, step by step

You'll do all of this once, from your iPhone. Follow along — most families finish in well under ten minutes.

1 Sign in with Apple

The only way to sign in — no password to remember, no email required. Apple gives the app a private identifier and nothing else unless you choose to share it.

⭐ Kids never sign in. They're profiles inside your family, picked from a list on the iPad.

2 Name your family

This shows at the top of the dashboard — “The Hansens,” “Familien Hansen,” whatever you like. You can change it later in Settings.

3 Add your kids

For each child: a name, an emoji avatar (or a photo), and two things that make the magic work — a goal (stars needed before a trophy, default 15) and a reward in your own words, like “Movie night with pizza.”

⭐ Each profile tracks its own stars and tasks independently — kids can't share one profile.

4 Set a parent PIN

A 4-digit code locks parent mode so kids can't wander into settings from the iPad. Face ID / Touch ID is offered automatically as a faster unlock. A banner reminds you until this is done.

⚠️ There's no “forgot PIN” flow yet — you reset it from Settings → Parent PIN while signed in. Keep it somewhere safe.

5 Connect the iPad

Sign the iPad into the same Apple ID, or share your family with iOS Family Sharing. The iPad then runs the always-on dashboard for the kids — covered in Part 2 below.

⭐ Don't see your kids on the iPad? Make sure both devices use the same Apple ID and the app is installed on both.
Part 1 · continued

Add routines and tasks

This is where the day takes shape. Our advice: start from the defaults and tweak — don't build from scratch.

📋

Categories & routines

Settings → Profiles & categories

Morning and Evening routines come pre-filled. Toggle them on/off and edit their steps — the checklist your interactive Routine Runner walks through.

  • ★ Categories are just labels for where a star came from
  • ★ Edit steps any time; no data is lost
  • ★ Run them together in Admin → Routines
✅

Tasks

Admin → Manage tasks

Single actions under a category. Pick a type and, if you like, limit it to certain weekdays.

  • ★ Recurring — once per day per kid, resets overnight
  • ★ One-off — stays until you delete it
  • ★ Weekday limits, e.g. “Pack school bag” on weekdays only
🌙

What “resets overnight” means

At midnight, daily tasks and routine progress clear so the slate is fresh each morning. Stars already earned stay on the board — only the to-do state resets.

Part 2 · On the iPad

The kids' dashboard

The iPad is a calm, always-on screen the kids actually want to look at. There's no login and nothing to break — they just tap.

Three swipeable pages

The dashboard pages between Stars (board + trophies), Tasks (StarTap), and Family (photos, weather, shared calendar). Nothing to configure — it just shows.

Tap when done

A kid picks their profile, taps a finished task, and it goes pending (green) for your approval. Rejected tasks turn red with “Try again” and can be retried the same day.

🔄

Optional: the Morning Round (a Cycle)

Set up a shared morning check-in from Admin → Morning Round. Each kid checks in across a sequence of categories; everyone moving in sync earns bonus stars — the one place stars are credited without approval. Great for school mornings.

Nice extras

Calendar & weather — zero setup

The Family page shows your shared calendar and local weather automatically. Nothing to configure inside the app.

📅

Shared family calendar

Upcoming events read straight from the iPad's iOS Calendar. Just make sure a calendar named “Family” (or “Familie”) exists and the app has Calendar access in Settings → Privacy → Calendars. Works with iCloud, Google or Outlook accounts added to iOS.

☀️

Local weather

Pulled from Apple Weather using approximate location — we never see or store where you are. Note: weather only appears on a real device, not the simulator (an Apple WeatherKit limitation, not a bug).
