FamilyHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026

FamilyHub ("the app", "we", "us") is a shared family dashboard that helps parents encourage children's daily routines through a star-reward system. We designed FamilyHub to collect as little personal data as possible, and to never use your data for advertising or tracking. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who controls your data

The data controller is the individual operator of FamilyHub. For any privacy question or request, contact magbjurling@gmail.com.

2. Accounts and who signs in

Only a parent or guardian creates an account, using Sign in with Apple. We receive a unique Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and an email address (which may be a private Apple relay address). Children do not sign in or have accounts. A child exists in FamilyHub only as a profile created and managed by the parent (a first name or nickname, an age, an emoji, and reward text that the parent types).

3. What we collect and why

  • Account data (Apple user ID, optional name/email) — to authenticate the parent and link the family's data to the account.
  • Family content (profile names, ages, emojis, star counts, categories, routine steps, reward text) — to provide the core features and sync them across the family's devices in real time.
  • Subscription status — to know whether premium features are active. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment card details.
  • Google Calendar data (optional) — only if a parent chooses to connect a Google account. We request read-only access (calendar.readonly) solely to display the day's events on the dashboard. We do not modify, share, or store calendar events beyond a short-lived cache needed to show today's items.
  • Approximate location (optional) — used on-device to show local weather and an outfit suggestion. We do not store your location history.

4. Children's privacy

FamilyHub is intended for use by parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Information about a child is entered by the parent and is minimized by design — a first name or nickname is sufficient, and no child login, photo, or contact information is required. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including the EU GDPR (and national child-data provisions) and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). A parent can edit or delete a child's profile at any time within the app.

5. How your data is stored

FamilyHub uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions) as its backend. Data is stored in Google Cloud data centres in the European Union. Access is restricted by security rules so that only the authenticated parents of a family can read or write that family's data. Google Calendar access tokens, if you connect a calendar, are stored server-side and are never exposed to client devices.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not display advertising.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not build advertising profiles of you or your children.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app (Google Firebase as our hosting/backend processor, and Apple for authentication and subscription processing), and only as required to provide the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual profiles in the app at any time. To delete your entire account and all associated family data, use the in-app delete option or email magbjurling@gmail.com; we will erase your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Under the GDPR our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the app) and your consent (e.g. for optional calendar and location features). To exercise any right, contact magbjurling@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Disconnecting Google Calendar

You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time inside the app, which revokes our access and deletes the stored token. You can also review and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. FamilyHub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be communicated in the app.

12. Contact

Questions? Email magbjurling@gmail.com.
