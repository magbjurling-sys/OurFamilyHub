Privacy Policy

We collect as little data as possible and never use it for advertising or tracking.

🔒
OurFamilyHub
Last updated: June 2026 · App Store ID 6775283053
OurFamilyHub is designed to collect as little personal data as possible and to never use your data for advertising or tracking.

1. Who controls your data

The data controller is the individual operator of OurFamilyHub. For any privacy question or request, contact magbjurling@gmail.com.

2. Accounts and who signs in

Only a parent or guardian creates an account, using Sign in with Apple. We receive a unique Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and an email address (which may be a private Apple relay address).

Children do not sign in or have accounts. A child exists in OurFamilyHub only as a profile created and managed by the parent — a first name or nickname, an emoji or optional photo, and a reward text that the parent writes.

3. What we collect and why

  • Account data (Apple user ID, optional name/email) — to authenticate the parent and link the family's data to the account.
  • Family content (profile names, emojis, star counts, categories, routine steps, reward text) — to provide the core features and sync them across devices in real time.
  • Subscription status — to know whether premium features are active. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment card details.
  • Calendar data (optional) — if you have added a calendar account to your device's iOS Settings, we read upcoming events directly on-device using Apple's EventKit framework. No calendar data is ever sent to our servers.
  • Approximate location (optional) — used on-device by Apple's WeatherKit to show local weather. We do not store your location or location history.
  • Profile photos (optional) — stored in Firebase Storage in the EU. You can delete them by removing the profile.

4. Children's privacy

OurFamilyHub is intended for use by parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Information about a child is entered by the parent and is minimised by design — a first name or nickname is sufficient.

We comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including the EU GDPR and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). A parent can edit or delete a child's profile at any time within the app.

5. How your data is stored

OurFamilyHub uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Storage) as its backend. Data is stored in Google Cloud data centres in the European Union (Finland). Access is restricted by security rules so that only the authenticated parents of a family can read or write that family's data.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not display advertising.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not build advertising profiles of you or your children.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app (Google Firebase as our hosting/backend processor, and Apple for authentication and subscription processing), and only as required to provide the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual child profiles in the app at any time. To delete your entire account and all associated family data, email magbjurling@gmail.com. We will erase your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Under the GDPR our legal bases are performance of a contract (providing the app) and your consent (e.g. for optional calendar and location features). To exercise any right, contact magbjurling@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be communicated in the app.

11. Contact

Questions? Email magbjurling@gmail.com.
